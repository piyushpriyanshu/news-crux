**News Crux 1.9.3: a slimmer top on the home page**

**One-line date bar**
- The date strip and the big date box are gone. One bar now shows the day, with ‹ and › to go back and forward one day at a time.
- Stories start much higher on the screen.
- The status chips (Final, All checked, catch-up) sit in one row under the bar.
- The news window and source count moved to the foot of the edition.

**Calendar**
- Tap the date to open a month calendar and jump straight to any day.
- A dot marks each day that has an edition on the phone. Use ‹ › to change month.
- "Latest edition" takes you back to the newest day.

**Reading progress**
- The date bar stays at the top while you scroll.
- A thin line under it fills as you read through the stories and shrinks when you scroll back up.
- The bar shows which story you are on, for example "Story 3 of 14".

**GS-wise view (optional)**
- Settings → Appearance → GS-wise view.
- Off by default. When on, the edition shows story counts for GS-I to GS-IV. Tap one to see only that paper's stories, and tap it again to show all.

**Sync progress on the logo**
- While a sync runs, the C in the logo fills along its curve. The header line still says what the sync is doing.

**No needless sync when you open the app**
- If the last sync worked and is less than an hour old, opening the app shows the saved news at once without syncing again.
- It still syncs on open after the morning edition time if the last sync came before it, and after 10 minutes if the last sync reached no source.
- Pull down or tap ⟳ to sync any time.
