**News Crux 1.7.3**

**Exam news is out of the edition**
- Posts about the exam itself no longer become cards. This covers UPSC notifications and calendars, results, toppers, strategy, and "how Mains questions are designed".
- Answer-writing practice, quizzes and MCQ posts are gone from Edition and Live.
- Daily roundup posts ("INSIGHTS Daily Current Affairs …") and "Latest News" link lists are gone too.
- Single lines are removed when they only describe a post or list links ("→ UPSC 2027 Notification …", "Check … list here").
- News about the bodies themselves stays, for example a new UPSC Chairperson or the Public Examinations Act.
- Editions you already have are cleaned when you open them.

**"Asked in Prelims" opens on older cards**
- Cards built before 1.7.0 now find their full question again. Tapping a year opens the question with the answer marked.
- A year whose full text isn't in the app (CDS/CAPF rows) now tells you so. Before, it did nothing.
