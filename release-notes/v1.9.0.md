**News Crux 1.9.0: sturdier news reading**

**Reads articles the reliable way first**
- Each article is read from the clean copy the publisher gives search engines, or the data built into the page. The visible layout comes second, and the old "biggest text block" method is the last resort.
- Every attempt is scored. Menus, "Also read" lines and promotions are dropped, and the best result wins.
- PIB, PM India and RBI releases now give their full official text. Before, the app got nothing from those pages.
- PDFs and image files are no longer read as articles.

**Fixed: Google News links lost part of their address**
- Links that came through Google News were cut at the first "=", so pages like PIB and RBI releases opened the wrong page or failed. They now arrive complete.

**More news found**
- News sitemaps from The Hindu, Hindustan Times, Economic Times, Business Standard, Down To Earth and Mint list every article from the last day. Only the sections that matter are kept.
- New direct feeds: Down To Earth, Vajiram and Mint.
- Removed four dead feeds: PIB HQ, PM India's feed, ET Top Stories, and Moneycontrol's economy feed, which stopped updating in 2024.
- Hindi-only headlines are skipped. PIB's "English" feed currently carries Hindi releases; the English ones still come through Google News.

**Figures you can trust more**
- A small ✓ beside a figure means two outlets, or an official release, give the same number.
- When sources disagree and one of them is official (PIB, RBI and others), the warning now says which is the official one.

**A daily check on GitHub**
- Every morning at 4:47 AM IST, GitHub reads every source the way the app does.
- If a source breaks, it opens an issue titled "Source check" in the repository, and closes it again once the source recovers.
