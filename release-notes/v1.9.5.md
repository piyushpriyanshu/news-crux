**News Crux 1.9.5: About boxes stay on the story**

**What was wrong**
- Some "About" boxes showed lines that had nothing to do with the story. For example, "Here: Here are the five food items…" under a food inflation story.
- The box took its subject from the first sentence in the article that looked like a definition, even when it was "Here are…" or "Retail inflation was the highest since…".
- Its other points could be definitions of different things from the same article.
- When the subject was too vague to check, the box was shown anyway.

**What changed**
- Only real definitions count. "X is a…", "X was established in…" qualify; "X was the highest…", "X said…", "Here are…" and lists ending in ":" do not.
- The subject must be named in the headline or the first lines of the story. An acronym (GRAP, LRS, SCO) may be named anywhere on the card.
- Every point in the box must be about that same subject. Points about anything else are removed.
- When there is nothing clear to check against, no About box is shown. No box is better than a wrong one.
- A coaching note's background with a vague heading ("Awards") now uses the note's title as the subject.
- The AI's About box must also name something on the card.

**Older editions and Live**
- The same checks run when you open any saved edition, and on the Live tab. Random boxes from the last few days disappear without a new sync.

**Checked on real news**
- Run on 128 articles from today's papers: the only boxes kept were true definitions of the story's subject (SCO, LRS, IIPH Hyderabad). Coaching backgrounds were kept as before, 12 of 13.
