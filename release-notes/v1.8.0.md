**News Crux 1.8.0**

**Try the PYQ first, then see the answer**
- Tapping a year in "Asked in Prelims" now opens the question with its options only. The answer stays hidden.
- Tap an option to answer. The app then shows whether you were right, marks the correct option and opens the explanation.
- Rule out options first with the ✕ beside each one, the way you would in the exam.
- "Show answer" reveals it without trying, when you only want to read.
- The sheet tells you what the question asked about and which news story it links to.
- When a story has several PYQs, "Next question" and "Previous" take you through all of them, with a running score.

**Your attempts are remembered**
- Years you have tried are marked on the card: ✓ right, ✗ missed. A question you open again shows how you did last time.
- Attempts are kept in your backups.
