**News Crux 1.9.1: CDS and CAPF questions in full**

- 1,612 CDS and CAPF questions (2020–2025) now open in full, the same way as the CSE ones. You get the question and options first, then the answer after you try, plus "Next question", ruling out options and your ✓/✗ marks.
- Each one shows its exam and paper, for example "CDS (II) 2023".
- They link to news stories the same way as before: "Asked in Prelims" shows them with "(CDS)" or "(CAPF)" when the CSE papers have little on a topic.
- Their answers come from the PW solutions and have not yet been checked against UPSC's answer key. The question sheet says so.
- 128 questions whose table or code grid did not come through in the source file stay as topic links only.
- To get the explanations for these questions, restore the new private explanations file in Settings → Restore. It includes the CSE explanations too.
