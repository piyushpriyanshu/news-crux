**News Crux 1.9.2: every UPSC paper on every story**

**All UPSC exams, linked to the news**
- Each card now lists every related past question from all UPSC papers in the app: Civil Services Prelims, CDS, CAPF, NDA, Combined Geo-Scientist, EPFO and Engineering Services. They are grouped by paper.
- If an Election Commission story is in the news, you see every question that named the Election Commission, from each paper.
- Long lists show six rows first, then "Show all".
- Rows from other papers are labelled with what the question asked.
- Cards built before this update get the full list when you open them.

**Every listed question opens**
- 3,363 questions from the other papers (CDS, CAPF, NDA, Geo-Scientist, EPFO, ESE; 2020–2025) open in full, like the CSE ones: try first, then the answer.
- 32 questions whose options were lost in the PDF's layout, and 45 match-the-list questions whose tables or code grids were lost, were rebuilt from the page images.
- 18 CSE questions with missing statements or pairs (mostly 2024–25) now have their full text.

**One card per body per day**
- When the same body is in the news more than once on the same day, those stories now become one card. For example, three Election Commission stories make one card, with the others under "More on … today".
- The card's past questions are combined, with each question listed once.
- This only applies within a single day. The same body in tomorrow's news gets its own card.
- Catch-all bodies such as the Supreme Court or the UN are not merged this way.
